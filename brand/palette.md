# PekSoft Radio Browser — Brand Palette

Derived from the PekSoft Radio Browser brand mark and visual identity.

## Core Brand Colours

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep Navy | `#0A0F1C` | Primary dark-mode canvas background |
| Midnight Navy | `#101A2E` | Dark-mode card / elevated surface & app icon tile |
| Signal Amber | `#FFB020` | Primary brand accent (radio waves, active play, dark-mode CTAs) |
| Deep Amber | `#B45309` | Light-mode accessible accent text & icons (WCAG AA on `#F6F4EE`) |
| Crisp Ice White | `#F4F6FB` | Dark-mode primary text & logo mark `P` |
| Warm Cream | `#F6F4EE` | Light-mode canvas background |

## Accessibility Notes (WCAG 2.2 AA)
- **Dark theme**: `#F4F6FB` on `#0A0F1C` (~16.5:1) and `#FFB020` on `#0A0F1C` (~10.3:1) both exceed AAA contrast.
- **Light theme**: `#0A0F1C` on `#F6F4EE` (~17.5:1) exceeds AAA. For amber text/links on light backgrounds, use Deep Amber (`#B45309`, ~5.0:1 on `#F6F4EE`) rather than `#FFB020`.
